gutter: 24px\n---\n\n## Brand & Style\nThe design system is engineered for a high-trust government environment, facilitating the bridge between ambitious students and public service. The brand personality is authoritative yet accessible, embodying the stability of a state institution with the modern efficiency of a technology-forward platform.\n\nThe visual style follows a **Corporate / Modern** aesthetic with **Minimalist** influences. It prioritizes clarity, systematic organization, and a sense of \"digital permanence.\" By utilizing a restricted, high-contrast palette and generous whitespace, the interface minimizes cognitive load for users navigating complex application processes. The influence of Shadcn UI brings a refined, component-driven architecture that feels contemporary and reliable.\n\n## Colors\nThis design system utilizes a palette rooted in \"Trust Blue\" to signify institutional authority. \n\n- **Primary (Deep Blue):** Used for core navigational elements, primary actions, and headers. It establishes the foundational tone of professionalism.\n- **Secondary (Light Blue):** Applied to active states, progress indicators, and informative highlights.\n- **Accent (Elegant Gold):** Reserved for high-importance focal points, such as \"Selected\" statuses, featured opportunities, and subtle decorative borders that denote \"premium\" or \"official\" quality.\n- **Neutrals:** A combination of White and Slate-50 creates a tiered background system. White is used for interactive surfaces (cards, inputs), while Slate-50 provides a soft contrast for the base page background.\n\n## Typography\nThe design system exclusively employs **Public Sans**, a typeface designed specifically for government interfaces. It provides exceptional legibility and a neutral, institutional tone.\n\nHeadlines use tighter letter spacing and heavier weights to command attention and establish hierarchy. Body text is optimized for long-form reading, essential for reviewing internship descriptions and legal terms. Label styles are used for metadata, form captions, and small UI indicators, utilizing a slightly increased letter spacing for clarity at small scales.\n\n## Layout & Spacing\nThe layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy for desktop to maintain a structured, document-like feel. \n\n- **Desktop:** A 12-column grid with a maximum width of 1280px. Content is centered with 24px gutters.\n- **Tablet:** An 8-column fluid grid with 24px margins.\n- **Mobile:** A 4-column fluid grid with 16px margins.\n\nThe spacing rhythm is based on a 4px baseline, ensuring all vertical and horizontal gaps are multiples of 4. This creates a predictable and rigorous alignment that reinforces the institutional nature of the portal. Large sections are separated by 48px (xl) to allow the design to \"breathe\" despite its formal structure.\n\n## Elevation & Depth\nThis design system utilizes **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Outlines** rather than heavy shadows. Depth is communicated through subtle shifts in surface color.\n\n- **Level 0 (Background):** Slate-50.\n- **Level 1 (Cards/Surface):** White with a 1px solid border (#e2e8f0).\n- **Level 2 (Dropdowns/Popovers):** White with a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)) and a 1px border.\n- **Accent Detail:** Featured cards or \"active\" items may use a 2px left-border or top-border in Elegant Gold (#f59e0b) to denote importance without breaking the flat, modern aesthetic.\n\n## Shapes\nThe design system uses **Soft** roundedness. A radius of 0.25rem (4px) is the standard for buttons, input fields, and small components. This provides a subtle modern touch while remaining serious and disciplined. \n\nLarger containers like cards use 0.5rem (8px) to soften the overall layout. Avatars and certain status \"pills\" may use a full-round (9999px) radius, but these are exceptions to the otherwise geometric rule.\n\n## Components\nConsistent styling across components ensures a unified user experience:\n\n- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are solid Deep Blue (#1e3a8a) with white text.
